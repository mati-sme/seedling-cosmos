How to play

Runs. Each run is a short timed burst. Tap the glowing targets before they fade. Chain hits to build a combo multiplier, dodge purple spores, and use your active abilities.

Collect. When time runs out you get a summary. Collect your Stardust and spend it in the Garden map: every node is a permanent upgrade (longer runs, bigger yield, new zones and abilities).

Rebloom & Supernova. Once the garden is grown, Rebloom for Seeds of Memory and a second map of permanent perks. Much later, Supernova for Star Cores and the Constellation.
